  tree, pine, bush, flower, reeds, rock, stump, birch, willow, berrybush, fern, wheat, log,
  roundtree, tallpine, tallgrass, corn, boulder, cherry.
  Convertidos de OBJ a GLB.
- Kenney, "Nature Kit" (https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit):
  mushroom, pumpkin, fence, logpile, campfire, tent, planter, sign, stones, canoe, carrots,
  melon, column, stonehead, menhir, boardwalk, palm, cactus.
- Kenney, "Fantasy Town Kit" (https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit):
  fountain, cart, stall. Textures/colormap.png es su textura, desaturada y entibiada.
